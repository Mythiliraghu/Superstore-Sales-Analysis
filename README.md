# Superstore Sales Analysis – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project explores the Superstore Sales dataset using Python to understand sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product profitability, and discount impact.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Examine regional sales performance.
- Understand customer segment contributions.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Key Business Insights
1. Technology generated the highest sales and profit among product categories.
2. The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
3. The Consumer segment contributed the most sales and profit.
4. Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies recorded negative profits.
5. Higher discount levels were associated with lower profitability.
6. November recorded the highest aggregate sales across calendar months.

## Conclusion
The analysis demonstrates how exploratory data analysis can help businesses identify sales patterns, understand profitability, and make data-driven business decisions.
